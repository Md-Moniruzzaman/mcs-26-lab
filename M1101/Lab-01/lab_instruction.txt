Exercise 1.1 – Encrypt and decrypt
    a) Encrypt the message "cryptography is fun" using k = 7. Show the
    numeric calculation for each letter.
    b) Decrypt the result and verify you get the original text back.
    c) Repeat with k = 19 (rot-19) and verify symmetry.

Exercise 1.2 – Brute-force attack
    You receive the following ciphertext (encrypted with an unknown shift):
            MYXQVBKX ZKBDEOKX LOXGXXKVBOBK
    a) Run the brute-force function on it and identify the key.
    b) What is the plaintext?
    c) How many operations did the brute-force require? Would this be feasible by hand?

Exercise 1.3 – Frequency analysis

    Download any English text of at least 500 characters (e.g., a Wikipedia para-graph).

    a) Encrypt it with a key of your choice.
    b) Plot the letter-frequency histogram of the ciphertext.
    c) Use find key frequency() to recover the key automatically.
    d) Report the recovered key and the χ2 scores for all 26 candidates.
